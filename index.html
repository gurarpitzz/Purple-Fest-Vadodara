ABOUT PURPLE FEST VADODARA

A National Movement Towards an Inclusive India

The Purple Fest is India's flagship festival of inclusion, designed to reshape societal perspectives, remove physical and attitudinal barriers, and illuminate the extraordinary potential of Persons with Disabilities.

Following monumental editions in Goa and New Delhi, Vadodara — the cultural heart of Gujarat renowned for the progressive vision of Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad III — now opens its arms to host this historic gathering. Across three unforgettable days at Navlakhi Ground and Sayaji Baug, experience a world where accessibility is not an afterthought, but the very foundation.

Dignity & Equal Opportunity

Advancing accessibility as a fundamental human right across education, jobs, and social life.

Sensitization & Empathy

Enabling non-disabled citizens to immerse themselves in lived experiences through interactive simulation zones.

Innovation & Independence

Connecting innovators, investors, and Divyang users to accelerate affordable assistive technology.

3 Days
Uninterrupted celebration, sports, culture & advocacy in Vadodara

EXPERIENCE THE FESTIVAL

6 Electrifying Purple Zones

Each zone is curated to delight, educate, empower, and inspire visitors of all abilities.

Technology & Innovation

Purple Assistive Tech Expo

Step into tomorrow. Experience cutting-edge AI Braille tablets, mind-controlled bionic prosthetics, eye-tracking speech synthesizers, and smart motorized wheelchairs developed by India's top IITs and startups.

  • Hands-on gadget trial stations
  • Live assistive tech hackathon & product launches
  • Affordable assistive aids consultation kiosk
Sports & Champions

Purple Sports Arena

Witness exhilarating athletic power and unyielding grit. Cheer for national teams in Blind Cricket, Wheelchair Basketball showdowns, Para-Badminton, Sitting Volleyball, and Boccia.

  • National Blind Cricket Championship match
  • Celebrity exhibition match with Gujarat cricket stars
  • Inclusive games workshop for kids and families
Arts & Performance

Purple Kalaa & Conclave Stage

Immerse yourself in soul-stirring live concerts by visually impaired symphonies, mesmerizing wheelchair Garba & contemporary dance troupes, deaf theater, and an inclusive couture fashion runway.

  • Vadodara's first Wheelchair Garba gala
  • Sign Language musical chorus & Deaf Poetry slam
  • Nightly celebrity gala performances
Sensory & Empathy

Purple Empathy Pavilion

Walk in another's shoes. Enter the Total Darkness Experience guided by visually impaired guides, navigate wheelchair obstacle courses, and learn everyday Indian Sign Language (ISL) in 15-minute micro sessions.

  • Blindfolded sensory dine-in counter
  • Sensory calming quiet pods for neurodivergent visitors
  • Interactive Braille printing workshop
Policy & Dialogue

Purple Think Tank Conclave

Over 60 national policymakers, corporate HR directors, accessibility architects, and disability advocates brainstorm inclusive employment policies, accessible public transport, and digital accessibility compliance.

  • Accessible Tourism in Gujarat Blueprint
  • Divyangjan Corporate Job Fair (1,000+ openings)
  • National Accessible India Campaign review
Crafts & Gastronomy

Purple Bazaar & Flavors of Gujarat

Support over 80 micro-entrepreneurs and artisans with disabilities selling handcrafted textiles, pottery, organic soaps, and artwork. Relish Vadodara’s authentic culinary delights with tactile and audio menus.

  • 100% proceeds directly to Divyang artisans
  • Tactile and Braille restaurant menus
  • Sign-language friendly ordering kiosks

OUR SOLEMN PLEDGE

100% Barrier-Free Environment

Purple Fest Vadodara is constructed from the ground up to be a gold-standard benchmark in universal accessibility.

Wheelchair Navigable

Zero-step gentle ramps (1:12 slope), rubberized smooth pathways across lawns, and accessible toilet blocks every 100 meters.

Indian Sign Language (ISL)

60+ certified ISL interpreters deployed across all keynotes, stage concerts, seminar rooms, and help desks.

Tactile Paths & Braille

Comprehensive tactile guidance pavers leading to every zone, Braille maps at entrances, and tactile stall indicators.

Live Audio Description

Real-time FM and mobile audio stream describing visual aspects of dance, sports matches, and theatrical performances.

Quiet Sensory Rooms

Dedicated low-lit, sound-dampened sensory rest pods equipped with weighted blankets and noise-cancelling headphones.

Personal Purple Buddies

500+ trained collegiate and NGO volunteers ready at the arrival gates to offer one-on-one sighted guide and mobility assistance.

Need special accommodations or accessible transit from Vadodara railway station? Our dedicated Accessibility Concierge is available 24/7.
